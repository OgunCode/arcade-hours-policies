Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

This policy covers the Yoyohiku Arcade Hours Twitch extension and these policy pages, operated by Yoyohiku (GitHub: OgunCode).

Schedule information

The broadcaster can enter daily stream titles, start times, end times, and off days. These settings are saved in Twitch’s Extension Configuration Service and supplied to the extension so viewers can see the schedule. Schedule entries are intended to be public; do not include private information in them.

Twitch authorization

Twitch’s Extension Helper supplies an authorization token and channel/session information. The configuration page reads the broadcaster role from that token to enable editing; Twitch handles authorization for saving settings. The extension does not request identity linking and does not send tokens or viewer identifiers to a developer-operated server.

Information we do not collect

This version has no developer-operated backend, analytics, advertising trackers, or payment processing. Its own code does not set cookies or use browser local storage. It does not collect chat messages, email addresses, passwords, payment information, or viewing histories. It does not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

Twitch and GitHub

Twitch hosts the extension assets, supplies the Extension Helper, and stores configuration. GitHub hosts these policy pages and any support issues. These providers may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs under their own policies: Twitch Privacy Notice and GitHub Privacy Statement. This policy does not replace theirs.

Retention and your choices

Schedule configuration remains with Twitch under its retention practices until replaced or otherwise removed by Twitch. Broadcasters can overwrite saved settings through Configure, clear both time fields to mark a day off, or deactivate the extension to stop displaying it. Deactivation is not a promise that Twitch deletes stored configuration. Contact Twitch for account or platform-held data requests. We maintain no separate viewer database to delete.

If you contact us through GitHub, the content you submit is stored by GitHub and may be public. You can manage your posts through GitHub’s controls and request assistance with project-owned content through the contact route below.

Children

This extension is intended for people permitted to use Twitch under Twitch’s age rules. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children. If you believe a support submission contains a child’s personal information, contact us without repeating that information publicly.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date when the extension’s data practices change.

Contact Yoyohiku (GitHub: OgunCode) through this repository’s Issues page. Issues are public: do not post passwords, tokens, personal identifiers, or sensitive information. If your request needs private details, first ask for a private contact method without including those details.
